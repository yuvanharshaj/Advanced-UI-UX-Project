## Test Protocol & Tasks

2. **Task 2**: Plan a trip from Majestic to Whitefield and submit.
3. **Task 3**: Open route details to inspect the stop timetable.
4. **Task 4**: Change app language from Settings.
5. **Task 5**: Turn on Larger text in Accessibility settings.
