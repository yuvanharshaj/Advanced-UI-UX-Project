# TransitOne - Usability Testing Record Template (v2.0)

For conducting in-person usability testing sessions with real users across English, Hindi, Kannada, and Urdu.

---

## 1. Test Protocol & Standard Tasks

1. **Task 1 (Onboarding)**: Select your native/preferred language on the welcome screen.
2. **Task 2 (Search & Combobox)**: Search for a route using the combobox (e.g. Majestic to Whitefield) or type a colloquial alias.
3. **Task 3 (Filter & Sort)**: Filter the available routes by Bus or Metro and sort by Cheapest or Fastest.
4. **Task 4 (Route Details & Timeline)**: Open route details to inspect the upcoming departures and stops timetable.
5. **Task 5 (Save & Share)**: Save the route to favourites and test the Share button.
6. **Task 6 (Ticket Flow)**: Adjust passenger count to 2, select payment method, and generate digital QR transit pass.
7. **Task 7 (Accessibility & Numerals)**: Turn on Larger text and switch between Latin digits and Native script numerals in Settings.
8. **Task 8 (Language Switch in Place)**: Change app language using the top-bar language chip and verify instant translation.

## 3. Post-Test System Usability Scale (SUS) Questions

1. I found the application easy to navigate in my native language. (1-5)
2. The route maps, arrows, and directional indicators felt natural for my script. (1-5)
3. The text size, line spacing, and fonts were clear and easy to read without overlapping. (1-5)
4. I felt confident purchasing a transit ticket and understanding the QR pass. (1-5)
5. The language switcher was quick and accessible without losing my place. (1-5)
